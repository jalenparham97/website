# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Next.js App Router with TypeScript, Bun, Tailwind CSS v4, and shadcn/ui.

## Users

Small business owners and teams looking for a reliable freelance partner to design and build a polished website.

## Product Purpose

Jalen Parham's freelance portfolio helps prospective clients understand the offer, assess the work, and start a project conversation. Success means a qualified visitor can understand the service quickly and book an introductory call.

## Positioning

A personable freelance web partner who combines thoughtful design with hands-on implementation, so small businesses can work with one person from first idea through a finished website.

## Operating Context

Visitors arrive from referrals, search, or direct outreach and scan the homepage on desktop or mobile before deciding whether to make contact.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The homepage must clearly communicate website design and development work, show selected proof, and provide a direct path to a call. The existing shadcn base-maia neutral theme is a binding color constraint. Do not invent testimonials, customer logos, metrics, prices, or project outcomes. A scheduling URL has not been provided.

## Brand Commitments

The site is for Jalen Parham. The voice should be clear, capable, warm, and personable. The interface should remain very minimalist and use the existing shadcn semantic color tokens rather than introducing a new palette.

## Evidence on Hand

The repository currently contains only create-next-app starter content and no verified portfolio imagery or case-study assets. Future work may use named projects only when supplied or already present in the source material.

## Product Principles

- Make the offer understandable in seconds.
- Let real work and concrete capabilities earn trust.
- Feel like a thoughtful person, not a generic agency.
- Keep the path to contact obvious and low-friction.
- Prefer clarity and restraint over decorative complexity.
