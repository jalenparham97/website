# Blog

## Scope & mode
Read surface at `/blog`: personable writing index. Visitor scans published pieces and opens one. Mode is Read, not Persuade.

## Audience & job
Prospective clients and peers who want to hear how Jalen thinks. Job: browse writing without a sales pitch or a featured-post hierarchy.

## Content & constraints
- Copy is first-person and personable. Not salesy. Not notes.
- Even two-up grid of all posts. No latest/featured section.
- Inherit site visual system (neutral tokens, zero radius, Geist, max-w-5xl inner column).
- Fallback copy matches seeded Sanity intro.

## Direction
Intro, then one grid. Every post is a peer.

## Memorable moment
Quiet equal cards. Date, title, excerpt, then Read.

Whether later posts need cover images. Add only when supplied.
